# Kenya statutory deductions for 2026 pay (PAY-01)

What the rule packs `KE-2025.02` and `KE-2026.02` (`database/rule-packs/`) hold, and where each figure comes from. Monthly pay. All figures are to be confirmed by the payroll accountant (BIZ-08) before the first pilot pay run.

| Item | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| PAYE bands (monthly taxable pay) | 10% to 24,000; 25% to 32,333; 30% to 500,000; 32.5% to 800,000; 35% above | Income Tax Act, Third Schedule, as amended by the Finance Act 2023 (from 1 July 2023) |
| Personal relief | 2,400 a month | Income Tax Act s.30 and Third Schedule |
| NSSF, pay dates to 31 Jan 2026 (Year 3, `KE-2025.02`) | 6% employee and 6% employer; Tier I to 8,000, Tier II from 8,000 to 72,000 | NSSF Act 2013 s.20, Year 3 from February 2025 |
| NSSF, pay dates from 1 Feb 2026 (Year 4, `KE-2026.02`) | 6% employee and 6% employer; Tier I to 9,000, Tier II from 9,000 to 108,000 | NSSF Act 2013 s.20, Year 4 from February 2026 |
| SHIF | 2.75% of gross pay, at least 300 | Social Health Insurance Act 2023; Social Health Insurance (General) Regulations 2024 |
| Affordable Housing Levy | 1.5% of gross pay from the employee and 1.5% from the employer | Affordable Housing Act 2024 s.4 |
| Taken before PAYE | NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy | Tax Laws (Amendment) Act 2024, from 27 December 2024 (SHIF and the levy; the 15% housing relief ended) |
| Pension deduction cap | 30,000 a month, NSSF included | Income Tax Act s.22A, as amended by the Tax Laws (Amendment) Act 2024 |
| Mortgage interest (own home) | up to 30,000 a month | Income Tax Act s.15(3)(b), as amended by the Tax Laws (Amendment) Act 2024 |
| Post-retirement medical fund | up to 15,000 a month | Income Tax Act s.22D, Tax Laws (Amendment) Act 2024 |
| Insurance relief | 15% of qualifying premiums, at most 5,000 a month | Income Tax Act s.31 |
| Non-cash benefits | not taxed if 5,000 a month or less in total; all of it taxed above | Income Tax Act s.5(4)(a), as amended by the Tax Laws (Amendment) Act 2024 |
| NITA | 50 per employee a month, from the employer | Industrial Training (Training Levy) Order 2007 |
| Rounding | each line to the cent, half away from zero | Our choice; to confirm with the accountant what KRA, SHA and NSSF expect |

Worked check: KES 100,000 gross in 2026 (from February) gives NSSF 6,000, SHIF 2,750, Housing Levy 1,500, taxable pay 89,750, PAYE 19,308.35 (21,708.35 less 2,400 relief) and net 70,441.65.
